# **App Name**: Brave Guides

## Core Features:

- Mentor Profiles: Display profiles of local mentors in South Africa, including their qualifications, experience, and areas of expertise.
- Mentor Search and Filtering: Allow users to filter and search for mentors based on specific criteria, such as location, language, or areas of expertise.
- User Dashboard: Enable authenticated users to view mentor profiles, send messages, and manage bookings through a personalized dashboard.
- AI Travel Assistant: Generate personalized travel tips and safety recommendations based on user's travel plans and interests, using a generative AI tool that accesses current events to make its recommendations.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Earthy green (#388E3C) to convey safety and reliability.
- Secondary color: Warm orange (#FF9800) to represent adventure and excitement.
- Accent: Sky blue (#2196F3) to suggest trust.
- Clear and legible typography to enhance readability and trust.
- Use simple and recognizable icons to aid navigation and understanding.
- Clean and organized layout to ensure ease of use and a professional feel.
